About

GHOST NYC is a bicycle safety intelligence layer built on the VAST video stack.

  • Footage: first-person NYC bike GoPro clips, camera nyc_bike_gopro-1.
  • Detections: YOLO11 sidecars from the pipeline. GHOST never adds boxes.
  • Tracking: ByteTrack-style association inside each clip.
  • Risk Score: a transparent 0–100 heuristic from path overlap, apparent proximity, closing motion, and timing.
  • Language: W&B serverless inference restates computed evidence. It cannot add observations.

What the numbers mean

Observations are YOLO boxes and tracks. Predictions are image-space projections of those tracks. Uncertainty is shown as amber. No value is a distance in meters or a measured crash probability.
